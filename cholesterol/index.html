From Routine to Baseline

Cholesterol Testing: history, value, global reality, limits, and future use.

From Routine to Baseline: Cholesterol Testing is a HeartFirst evidence review on the history, value, global use, limitations, and future role of standard cholesterol testing.

Core argument

A standard cholesterol test remains one of cardiovascular prevention’s most important foundations. But in modern risk assessment, it is best understood as a baseline lipid assessment, not a full cardiovascular risk picture.

What the report covers

  • How cholesterol testing became routine in many health systems.
  • Why the standard lipid panel remains clinically valuable and treatment‑enabling.
  • Where it may be incomplete, including ApoB, Lipoprotein(a), metabolic risk, family history, and global access gaps.
