About HeartFirst

Clearer heart-risk guidance, built for action.

HeartFirst by Shyntesy creates evidence-informed tools, briefings, and reports to help people clarify cardiovascular risk, prepare better health-team conversations, and take useful next actions.

HeartFirst exists because too many people are told their results are “normal” or “fine” without being helped to understand what was checked, what may still be unknown, and what action should come next.

Our mission

HeartFirst helps people clarify their heart‑risk picture, navigate health‑team conversations, and support prevention before avoidable heart attacks, strokes, valve disease, and other cardiovascular events occur.

What we create

Tools

Practical worksheets, checklists, and guided kits that help people organise what is known, unknown, and worth discussing.

Briefings

Short, focused summaries that make important cardiovascular risk topics easier to understand and share.

Reports

Evidence reviews and research‑informed publications for deeper clinical, policy, and public‑health context.

Glossary

Plain‑language and specialist‑level definitions of heart‑risk, testing, and prevention terms.

Our framework

HeartFirst follows a simple pathway: Clarify → Navigate → Prevent.

First, clarify what is known and unknown. Then, navigate the right conversations and decisions with your health team. Then, build prevention habits and follow‑up systems that support long‑term risk reduction.

Editorial standards

  • Evidence‑informed and medically cautious
  • Clear about what is known, uncertain, or emerging
  • Designed to support, not replace, qualified health professionals
  • Non‑alarmist, practical, and patient‑centred
  • Independent, ad‑free, and free from supplement‑style promises

What HeartFirst is not

HeartFirst is not a clinic, diagnostic service, emergency service, medical practice, or substitute for personalised care. Our resources are educational and organisational tools. Medical decisions belong with you and your health team.
