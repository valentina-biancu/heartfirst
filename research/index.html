How HeartFirst uses research

HeartFirst research is translated into practical tools, briefing cards, glossary entries, and patient-facing resources. The goal is not to overwhelm people with evidence, but to make the evidence usable.

Our working pathway is Clarify → Navigate → Prevent: clarify what is known and unknown, navigate health-team conversations and decisions, and support prevention through practical next actions.

Where available, HeartFirst research outputs may include DOI-linked records, downloadable PDFs, version notes, and related briefing materials.
