Stage explanation

Coronary supply context

The page begins with a coronary artery supplying a territory of working heart muscle.

What this means

A heart attack pathway matters because heart muscle needs continuous oxygen-rich blood. The visual starts with supply before showing what happens when that supply is threatened.

What this does not show

This is not your anatomy, not a symptom checker, and not a diagnosis. It does not show whether you are having a heart attack.

What heart attack risk can involve

  • A coronary artery supplying a heart-muscle territory
  • A blockage or severe flow reduction in that artery
  • Oxygen shortfall in downstream heart muscle
  • Emergency evaluation with symptoms, ECG, blood tests, and imaging

Discuss with your health team

Ask how your risk context is being interpreted before an emergency ever happens, including Lp(a), ApoB, blood pressure, metabolic risk, smoking exposure, inflammatory markers, calcium-score findings, symptoms, medicines, and family history.

This visualisation is educational. If symptoms suggest a heart attack or another emergency, seek urgent medical help immediately. Do not use this page to decide whether symptoms are safe.
