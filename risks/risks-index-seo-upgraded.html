Risk Atlas

Visual explainers for hidden and incomplete heart risk

The Risk Atlas shows one cardiovascular risk process at a time, then links processes through risk pathways to help you understand how these risks interact, and to support informed conversations with your health team and family.

Explore the atlas

Follow the pathway from plaque to events and valve risk

Start with the artery-wall foundation, then follow how biological activity, rupture, clotting, and blood-flow interruption can connect. The valve pathway is included separately because aortic valve calcification and aortic stenosis are not the same as artery plaque.

Plaque What plaque is, what it contains, and how it builds inside an artery wall. Inflammation How inflammation can affect artery walls, plaque behaviour, and cardiovascular risk context. Rupture How the fibrous cap over a plaque can break and expose plaque contents. Clot How blood can react at a rupture site and reduce flow. Cascade How plaque, inflammation, rupture, and clot can connect in an event pathway. Heart attack How blocked coronary blood flow can threaten heart muscle. Stroke How blocked brain blood flow can become an emergency pathway. Valve calcification How calcium can build up on aortic valve leaflets. Aortic stenosis How a narrowed valve can increase pressure load and strain the heart.

Educational scope: these visual explainers do not diagnose, predict, or treat any condition. They are designed to clarify risk pathways and prepare better health team conversations.
