Risk Atlas

Visual explainers for hidden and incomplete heart risk

The Risk Atlas shows one cardiovascular risk process at a time, then links processes through risk pathways to help you understand how these risks interact, and to enable informed conversations with your health team and family.

Suggested path

  • Start with Plaque to understand this foundational risk.
  • Continue to Inflammation for biological risk activity around the artery wall.
  • Continue to Rupture to see how a plaque cap can fail.
  • Continue to Clot to see how blood can react at a rupture site and reduce flow.
  • Continue to Cascade to see how plaque, inflammation, rupture, and clot can connect in an event pathway.
  • Continue to Heart attack to see how blocked coronary blood flow can threaten heart muscle.
  • Continue to Stroke to see how blocked brain blood flow can become an emergency pathway.
  • Continue to Valve calcification to see how calcium can build up on the aortic valve leaflets.
  • Continue to Aortic stenosis to see how a narrowed valve can increase pressure load and strain the heart.

Educational scope

Educational scope: these visual explainers do not diagnose, predict, or treat any condition. They are designed to clarify risk pathways and prepare better health team conversations. The Lp(a) Risk Atlas sits separately as a cross-cutting inherited risk signal.
