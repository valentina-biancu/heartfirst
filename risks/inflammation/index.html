This risk atlas shows how inflammation can affect artery walls and plaque behaviour over time. It helps make clear one process rather than trying to explain every cardiovascular pathway at once. What this means

This baseline view helps separate inflammation from vague alarm language. Inflammation here means immune cell involvement, chemical signalling, and vessel-wall irritation in and around the plaque environment.

What this does not show

This atlas does not tell you whether inflammation is present in your arteries, how active it is, or whether a plaque is unstable. It shows one educational process in isolation so the underlying structure is easier to understand.

What inflammation can involve

  • Immune cell movement into the artery wall
  • Inflammatory chemical signals
  • Endothelial irritation or dysfunction
  • More active plaque behaviour

Discuss with your health team

If your risk picture feels incomplete, ask what current checks do and do not show, and whether inflammatory context is relevant to discuss.

This visualisation is educational. It does not diagnose, predict, or treat any condition. It is not a substitute for advice from your health team. If symptoms suggest an emergency, seek urgent medical help.
