Briefings

Short, practical HeartFirst summaries for quick reference, sharing, and better health‑team conversations.

HeartFirst Briefings are short, focused resources designed to make complex cardiovascular risk topics easier to understand, discuss, and act on.

Briefings may include one‑page At a Glance cards, printable summaries, clinician‑facing briefs, policy notes, and practical explainers.

Topics being prepared

  • Cholesterol testing as a baseline, not always the full picture
  • Lipoprotein(a), or Lp(a), and inherited risk
  • ApoB, particle burden, and discordant lipid results
  • Heart‑risk clarity, family history, blood pressure, metabolic risk, and next actions
