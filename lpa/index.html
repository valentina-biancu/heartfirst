Lp(a)

Inherited risk. Often overlooked. Usually measured once.

Lipoprotein(a), or Lp(a), is an inherited cardiovascular risk marker linked with heart attack, stroke, and aortic stenosis. Many people with elevated Lp(a) have never been tested.

This HeartFirst resource is being prepared as a clear, evidence-based guide for people who want to understand Lp(a), what testing can show, and what to discuss with their health team.
