Heart Risk & Protection Glossary

Plain-language and specialist-level explanations of heart‑risk, testing, prevention, and protection terms.

The Heart Risk and Protection Glossary is being prepared as a definitive, freely accessible reference for cardiovascular risk and prevention terms.

Each entry will be written at three levels so readers can move from plain‑language understanding to deeper clinical context without getting lost.

Built in three levels

  • Birds‑eye: a short plain‑language explanation for first‑time readers.
  • Ground: practical context for patients and health professionals.
  • Deeper: specialist‑level detail for advanced readers and clinical reasoning.

Topics being added

  • Standard lipid panel, LDL‑C, HDL‑C, triglycerides, non‑HDL‑C
  • ApoB, Lipoprotein(a), inherited risk, family history
  • Blood pressure, metabolic risk, kidney function, inflammation, plaque burden
  • Heart attack, stroke, aortic stenosis, cardiovascular prevention

A starter set of entries is already drafted and will appear here shortly.
