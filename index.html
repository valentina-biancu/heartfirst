What we believe

The convictions that built this platform.

These are not values statements assembled in a workshop. They are the reasons HeartFirst exists in the form it does.

A prepared patient changes the clinical outcome
A person who arrives at a specialist appointment with organised records, a specific question, and the right clinical vocabulary gets a different conversation — and a different outcome — than one who arrives with a bag of lab reports and a vague worry. Preparation is not soft. It is clinical leverage.
The information exists. The structure does not.
Everything a person needs to understand their cardiovascular risk is published, peer-reviewed, and freely available. The problem is not access to information — it is the absence of a framework to make sense of it, identify what’s missing, and turn it into one clear next action.
Anxiety is not a conversion strategy
The people who come to HeartFirst are already dealing with real fear — about what they know, what they don’t know, and what they may have passed to the people they love. We will not add to that fear to drive a sale. We sell clarity and preparation, not alarm.
Prevention starts before any appointment
The small, consistent, evidence-based actions that reduce cardiovascular risk do not require a prescription or a diagnosis. They require knowledge of what works, the motivation to begin, and one specific action today. HeartFirst Momentum Actions exist for exactly this.
Inherited risk should not stay hidden
Lp(a) is almost entirely genetic. Familial hypercholesterolaemia is heritable. A person who discovers elevated inherited risk does not carry it alone — their siblings, children, and parents may share it. Every HeartFirst system includes the tools to begin those conversations.
Premium quality is a form of respect
People dealing with cardiovascular risk deserve products that take the subject as seriously as they do. Cheap design, vague copy, and unevidenced claims communicate that the product does not respect the situation. HeartFirst is built to the standard the subject demands.
Artificial & augmented intelligence

We use AI. We are not apologising for it.

AI is one of the most significant opportunities in the history of healthcare access. We are using it, and we are being completely transparent about how and why.

Our position — stated plainly

AI is not a shortcut.
It is an amplifier of human preparation.

HeartFirst was built in collaboration with AI — for research synthesis, content structure, educational framework design, and platform development. We consider this a feature, not a compromise. If that position is a dealbreaker for you, our products are probably not the right fit. For everyone else: read on.

AI for research synthesis
AI assists us in synthesising ACC/AHA, ESC/EAS, NICE, and Lp(a) consensus guidance into accessible educational frameworks. The evidence base is real. The synthesis is faster and broader than a single human team could achieve alone.
AI for structure and clarity
The Clarify → Navigate → Prevent framework, the Known / Unknown / To Discuss system, and the tool architecture were developed with AI collaboration. The result is more structured and more usable than what a single author would produce working alone.
AI for platform development
The website, product pages, legal documents, and technical infrastructure were built with AI assistance. This allows a small team to build and maintain a platform at a quality level that would previously have required a much larger one.
Human judgement leads, always
AI does not make clinical, editorial, or ethical decisions at HeartFirst. Every product claim, every piece of clinical content, and every ethical position is reviewed and owned by a human. AI is the amplifier. Humans are accountable.
Augmented intelligence, not artificial replacement
The goal of AI in HeartFirst is not to replace clinical expertise — it is to extend the reach of educational preparation to people who currently have no structured access to it. Augmented intelligence: human capability extended, not substituted.
Massive potential for patients and society
We believe AI-assisted health education is one of the most significant levers available to reduce the burden of preventable cardiovascular disease globally. The alternative — limiting access to those who can afford specialist time — is not acceptable to us.
To those who cannot accept any product involving AI: we respect that position and will not attempt to change it. HeartFirst is not the right product for you, and we would rather you know that clearly than feel misled after purchase. To everyone else — including the clinicians, researchers, patient advocates, and policymakers who understand what AI-assisted health education can do at scale — you are exactly who we built this for.

Global ambition

This platform is not built for one country.

Cardiovascular risk is a global problem. The information gap — between what medicine knows and what individuals can access and act on — exists in every healthcare system, at every income level, and across every culture. HeartFirst is built to be relevant wherever people are dealing with heart risk and not getting the clarity they need.

Whether you are in a universal NHS-style system, a private insurance market, or navigating care with no system at all — the need to understand your cardiovascular risk, prepare your questions, and know what to ask is universal. HeartFirst is designed to be useful regardless of what care looks like where you are.

HeartFirst is built for individuals — but we recognise that clinicians, practice nurses, patient advocates, and health educators also operate in environments where structured cardiovascular risk education tools are scarce. Professional and institutional licensing enquiries are welcome.
For researchers and policymakers
The intersection of AI, structured health education, and cardiovascular prevention is an active research frontier. For the long game
We are building a platform, not a product. How we operate

The standards we hold ourselves to.

These are not aspirations — they are the documented operating principles of HeartFirst, verifiable in every product, every page, and every interaction.

01
Evidence over opinion
Every clinical claim is grounded in published, peer-reviewed evidence from ACC/AHA, ESC/EAS, NICE, and major cardiovascular consensus bodies. We cite the evidence base, not a blog post or a trend.
02
Education, never diagnosis
The line between education and medical advice is not always obvious in health products. We hold it clearly and deliberately. Every product includes an explicit disclaimer. The Medical Disclaimer page exists for this reason.
03
No exploitation of fear
Countdown timers, false scarcity, and manufactured urgency are marketing tools that use anxiety as a weapon. Our users are often already frightened. We do not add to that to close a sale.
04
Privacy as a right, not a feature
We never sell personal data. We never share it with advertisers. We collect only what we need to deliver the product. Full GDPR compliance is documented and maintained — see our Privacy Policy.
05
Honest about AI
We use AI. We say so. We explain how and why. We do not embed AI assistance and hope nobody notices. Transparency about our methods is part of what it means to operate with integrity in a health-adjacent space.
06
The guarantee means the guarantee
The 30-Day Clarity Guarantee is unconditional. No hoops, no questions, no process beyond one email. If the product did not deliver what it promised, you get a full refund. The guarantee is a promise, not a marketing line.
