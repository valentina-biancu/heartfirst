The HeartFirst Mission
Why HeartFirst exists

Heart risk is common.
Practical prevention is not.

Cardiovascular disease is not just another health risk. It is the world’s leading cause of death, yet most people never see their full risk picture, understand the right questions, or know what to discuss with their health team.

HeartFirst gives you a step-by-step pathway to clarify, navigate, and reduce risk before heart attack, stroke, or another life-changing event forces the issue.
