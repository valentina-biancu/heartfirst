Five-stage experience

Designed around the work users need to finish.

Clarify should feel like a calm command centre: structured enough to reduce uncertainty, flexible enough to respect what people do not know yet.

01

Who needs clarity?

Separate the person, the helper, and the sharing context before health details are combined.

02

What do we know?

Capture known tests, risk factors, medicines, relevant history, and concerns once.

03

What is unclear?

Turn missing information into a practical list rather than a vague worry.

04

What needs discussion?

Prepare questions and priorities for a health team conversation.

05

What happens next?

Record agreed decisions, next actions, owners, dates, and follow-up for the people involved.
