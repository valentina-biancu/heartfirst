The pathway

Clarify. Navigate. Prevent.

The HeartFirst product pathway turns hidden or incomplete heart risk signals into clearer information, better health team conversations, and earlier prevention action.

Step 1 · Clarify

Heart Risk Clarity Check

For the moment when something feels incomplete: a cholesterol result, family history, Lp(a), ApoB, blood pressure, symptoms, calcium score, or questions that will not organise themselves.

Best when

You need to turn scattered information into a clean snapshot: known facts, missing context, questions to discuss, and one practical next action.

  • Organise results, risks, and records without drowning in detail.
  • Separate what is known, unknown, and worth discussing.
  • Prepare for a focused health team conversation.
Explore Clarity Check

Checkout and delivery happen on Shyntesy.

Step 2 · Navigate

Heart Risk Navigate

For the stage after the first snapshot, when the question becomes: what should I ask, what should I gather, what should I challenge, and what should I do next with my health team?

Best when

You have enough information to know this matters, but not enough structure to move through appointments, records, testing options, and decision points calmly.

  • Prepare health team conversations and portal messages.
  • Map possible tests, records, and decision points.
  • Reduce avoidable friction before appointments.
Explore Navigate

Use after Clarify, or start here if your risk picture is already partly organised.

Step 3 · Prevent

Heart Risk Prevent

For turning risk knowledge into prevention momentum: habits, monitoring, risk context, follow-through, and practical preparation over time.

Best when

You want to move from one-off concern to a steadier prevention system that supports long-term action without pretending to replace medical care.

  • Build a prevention routine around real risk context.
  • Track changes, questions, and follow-through over time.
  • Stay prepared as evidence, options, or life circumstances change.
Explore Prevent

Best for people ready to work beyond first-step clarity.

Choosing the right route

Start with the problem you are actually trying to solve.

Most people do not need more generic heart health information. They need to know what their current risk signal means, what is missing, and what action belongs next.

If this sounds like you, start here.

“I have results or family history, but no clear picture.”
Start with Heart Risk Clarity Check. It is designed to organise what you know before you try to solve everything.
“I need to prepare for a health team conversation.”
Start with Heart Risk Navigate. It is designed for questions, records, testing options, and appointment preparation.
“I know risk is present and need prevention follow-through.”
Start with Heart Risk Prevent. It supports prevention momentum after the first risk picture begins to form.
“I am not ready to buy anything.”
Use the free HeartFirst Clarity Check. It gives you a simple place to capture known facts, unanswered questions, and one next action.
How it works

From risk signal to prevention momentum.

HeartFirst products are not built to diagnose or prescribe. They are built to help people prepare, organise, and make better use of personalised care from qualified health professionals.

01
Capture
Put results, risk signals, family history, symptoms, records, and questions into a usable structure.
02
Clarify
Separate verified facts from missing information, uncertainty, and items worth raising with your health team.
03
Discuss
Turn scattered worry into better questions, clearer messages, and more productive conversations.
04
Act
Choose one useful next action, then build prevention follow-through around the risk picture you and your health team are developing.
