# Readme

## Heart Risk Clarity Check vs Heart Risk Navigator Starter

Note: need to change name from HeartFirst Clarity Check -> **Heart Risk Clarity Check**

Keep **Clarity Check**, but make it clearly a **smaller, faster, public-facing tool** than the **Heart Risk Navigator Starter**.

They are close, but not too close if you separate their roles properly.

## My recommendation

Use this ladder:

```text
1. HeartFirst Clarity Check
   Quick worksheet / web tool / low-friction entry point

2. Heart Risk Navigator Starter
   Printable starter pack / deeper guided tool / first structured step

3. Heart Risk Navigator System
   Full guided pathway / paid or fuller product ecosystem
```

The uploaded Clarity Check is already positioned as a “one-page preparation tool” taking 10–20 minutes, helping users organise what they know, what needs clarification, what to discuss, and one useful next action. That is a clean micro-tool role. 

## Best positioning difference

### Keep: **HeartFirst Clarity Check**

Use it for:

```text
Quick clarity before an appointment
After a confusing result
Before messaging a doctor / health team
Social media funnels
Toolkit freebie
SEO landing page
Low-friction trust builder
```

Suggested description:

```text
A quick worksheet to organise what you know, what needs clarification, what to discuss with your health team, and one useful next action.
```

### Keep: **Heart Risk Navigator Starter**

Use it for:

```text
A more complete printable starter
Opt-in download
First step into the Heart Risk Navigator System
Campaign / product funnel asset
PDF workbook
Structured onboarding tool
```

Suggested description:

```text
A structured starter tool to map your heart risk context, prepare better health team questions, and choose one clear next action.
```

## The key difference

The Clarity Check should feel like:

```text
“I need to get my thoughts and questions clear.”
```

The Heart Risk Navigator Starter should feel like:

```text
“I want a structured starting point for navigating my heart risk.”
```

That makes them complementary, not duplicate.

## One risk to avoid

Do not describe both with the same phrase:

```text
organise what you know, what is missing, what to discuss, and one next action
```

That phrase belongs more naturally to **Clarity Check**.

For the **Navigator Starter**, broaden slightly:

```text
map your heart risk context
identify gaps
prepare better health team questions
choose one clear next action
repeat the process
```

## Best channel split

I would use **Clarity Check** as the public/free tool on `/toolkit/clarity-check/`.

I would use **Heart Risk Navigator Starter** as a downloadable or funnel-linked resource, especially from:

```text
- social posts
- email opt-ins
- product pathway
- HeartFirst landing page CTA
- “start here” sections
```

## Final verdict

Do **not** ditch Clarity Check.

Keep it as the **quick public worksheet**.

Use **Heart Risk Navigator Starter** as the **deeper starter pack** and bridge into the full Heart Risk Navigator System.

The naming architecture works well:

```text
HeartFirst Clarity Check
↓
Heart Risk Navigator Starter
↓
Heart Risk Navigator System
```
